How Digital Signature Certificates Work

Simple, secure, and legally recognized digital authentication

A Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) is an electronic signature that proves the authenticity of the signer and secures your digital documents. DSCs are issued by Certifying Authorities (CAs) after verifying the identity of the applicant. They use a unique encryption key pair – a public key and a private key – to sign and verify documents.

Simple 4-Step Process

1

Choose DSC Type

Select Class 1, 2, or 3 based on your specific requirements and usage needs.

2

Submit Documents

Provide identity proof, address proof, and photograph for verification process.

3

Verification

Receive your DSC after successful verification by the Certifying Authority.

4

Install & Use

Install your DSC on a secure USB token or keystore, then e-sign on portals like MCA, GST, and e-tendering platforms.
